        \subsection{Kuhn}
            \lstinputlisting[language=C++]{src/graph/kuhn.cpp}

        Vertex cover of a graph --- a set of vertices such that every edge of the graph is incident to at least one vertex from the set.
        
        Let $M$ --- max matching. Mentally orient the edges of the graph: direct edges from $M$ from the right part to the left, and the rest --- from the left to the right, 
        then run a depth-first search from all vertices of the left part not included in $M$.
        The graph is partitioned into several sets: $L^{+}$, $L^{-}$, $R^{+}$, $R^{-}$, where the ``plus'' sets are the sets of vertices visited during the traversal. Then $V_{min} = L^{-} \cup R^{+}$.

        Independent set of vertices --- a set of vertices such that no pair of vertices is connected by an edge. The complement of a minimum vertex cover is a maximum independent set.

        Path cover of a DAG: $n - matching$
